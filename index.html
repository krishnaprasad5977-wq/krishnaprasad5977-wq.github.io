Common questions

FAQ

How much does a website cost?
A basic 3-page site starts at ₹5,000. An e-commerce or web app is typically ₹8,000–₹15,000. You'll get an exact quote after a 10-minute call — there are no hidden charges beyond what's agreed upfront.
How long does a project take?
A simple website takes 5–7 days. A multi-page site or basic app is 10–14 days. Complex apps with custom backend work are 3–4 weeks. Timeline is confirmed before you pay the advance.
I'm not technical at all — will this still work for me?
Yes. Most clients are not technical. Just tell me what your business does and what you want — I'll handle everything else and explain things in plain language at every step.
Do I own everything after delivery?
100%. All logins, source code, hosting credentials, and domain access are handed over on final payment. Nothing is locked to me — you can manage it yourself or with any other developer after delivery.
How does payment work?
50% advance to start, 50% on delivery after you review the finished work. Payments via UPI, GPay, PhonePe, or bank transfer. Tutoring courses are paid upfront per course.
How do the tutoring sessions work?
Start with a free 30-min intro call to align on your goals. Then 1–2 hour sessions, 2–3 times a week — ad-hoc or a fixed schedule, whichever works for you. Sessions are recorded on request.
