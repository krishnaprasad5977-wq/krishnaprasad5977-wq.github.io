Common questions

FAQ

The questions I get asked most before someone reaches out.

How much does a website cost?
A basic business website starts around ₹5,000–₹8,000. An e-commerce store or web app starts around ₹8,000–₹15,000. I'll give you an exact quote after a 10-minute call — no surprises.
How long does a project take?
A simple website takes 5–7 days. A multi-page site or basic web app takes 10–14 days. More complex apps can take 3–4 weeks. I'll give you a realistic timeline upfront.
I'm not technical — can I still reach out?
Absolutely. Most of my clients are not technical. Just tell me what your business does and what you want — I'll translate that into a solution and explain everything in plain language.
Do I own the website / app after delivery?
Yes — 100%. You get all logins, source code, hosting credentials, and domain access. Nothing is locked to me. You can hand it to anyone in the future.
What's the tutoring structure?
We start with a free 30-min intro call to understand your goals. Then we plan the sessions — typically 1–2 hours each, 2–3 times a week. Ad-hoc or structured program, your choice.
What payment methods do you accept?
UPI, GPay, PhonePe, or bank transfer. I take 50% advance to start work and the remaining 50% on final delivery. For tutoring, payment is per course, collected before the course begins.
